How to use

A distraction-free place to organize your lectures. Group YouTube videos into playlists, then watch them without recommendations, comments or sidebars.

1Create a playlist

  1. When you open the app, the middle of the page shows Create a playlist.
  2. Type a name, for example Linear Algebra or Week 3 Physics.
  3. Click Create (or press Enter). The new playlist appears in the Playlists list on the right and opens straight away.

You can make as many playlists as you like, for example one per subject or exam.

2Add videos

  1. Open a playlist by clicking it in the list on the right. It expands to show its videos.
  2. Paste a YouTube link or video ID into the bar at the top and click Add.
  3. The video appears in the playlist with its title and thumbnail. Adding a video never starts playback.

These formats all work:

  • https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID
  • https://youtu.be/VIDEO_ID
  • https://www.youtube.com/embed/VIDEO_ID, /shorts/VIDEO_ID and /live/VIDEO_ID
  • The bare 11-character video ID on its own
The bar is greyed out until a playlist is open, because videos are always added to the playlist you have open. A video can only be in a playlist once, but the same video can be in different playlists.

3Play a video

The player stays hidden until you pick something. Click any video in the open playlist and the player appears and starts playing it. Click the same video again to resume it if it is paused.

Clicking the video itself also plays or pauses it. When a video ends, the next one in the playlist starts automatically. After the last video you see an End of playlist screen with a Replay this video button, so YouTube never gets to show you suggestions.

6Manage your playlists

  • Start another playlist: click + New next to the Playlists heading to return to the create box.
  • Switch playlists: click a different one in the list. The player hides until you choose a video.
  • Remove one video: hover over it in the list and click the × on its right.
  • Empty a playlist: click Clear at the bottom of the open playlist. This removes its videos but keeps the playlist.
  • Delete a playlist: click the × on its row. You are asked to confirm if it contains videos.

7Your data is saved in your browser

Playlists are stored in your browser (localStorage) automatically, so they are still there after a refresh or restart. On a new visit you land on the create screen with all your playlists listed on the right.

Playlists live only in the browser you made them in. They do not sync between devices or browsers, and they are lost if you clear your site data or use a private/incognito window.

8Good to know

  • Ads: the app cannot block YouTube ads. If a video has ads, they still play. An ad blocker or YouTube Premium removes them.
  • Videos that will not play: some uploaders disable embedding. Those videos show an error in the player. Remove them and try another upload.
  • Titles show as "Video …": the title could not be fetched (for example when you are offline). The video still works.
  • Internet needed: videos stream from YouTube, so you need a connection to play them.

10Quick answers

My playlists disappeared.

Check that you are in the same browser and not in a private window, and that site data was not cleared. Playlists cannot be recovered once the browser data is gone.

The Add button is greyed out.

No playlist is open. Create one, or click an existing playlist on the right.

It says the link is not a YouTube link.

Copy the address from the browser's address bar or YouTube's Share button. Playlist and channel links are not supported, only single-video links.

I clicked a video and nothing played.

The uploader may have disabled embedding, or your browser may be blocking third-party content from YouTube. Try another video or turn off the blocker for this site.
